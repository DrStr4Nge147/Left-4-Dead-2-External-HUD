This Workshop item is the exporter half. It writes live survivor state to a file; the separate application reads that file and draws the HUD. Both halves are required and must be on the same version - currently v2.1.2.

[list]
[*][b]Standalone[/b] includes the required .NET components.
[*][b]Compact[/b] is smaller, but requires the [url=https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/9.0].NET 9 Desktop Runtime for Windows x64[/url]. Choose [b]Desktop Runtime[/b], not only the base .NET Runtime or ASP.NET Core Runtime.
[/list]

[h1]WHAT IT IS[/h1]

An external survivor HUD for oversized rosters. It shows every mortal survivor in the session, not only the four slots available in the built-in HUD. It is designed for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3766326051]Finale Soldiers[/url] and other mods that spawn more than four survivors.

Cards show the survivor name, permanent health, temporary health from pills or adrenaline, black-and-white state, and incapacitated / hanging / dying / dead state. Cards show each survivor's carried items, except your own: your throwable, kit, and pills sit with your weapons and ammunition instead, where vanilla keeps them. The Tab scoreboard shows the whole extra survivor team.

Your own weapons sit in their own panel: primary with its magazine and reserve, pistol / Magnum / chainsaw / melee below it, and your throwable, kit, and pills under those. Weapons use the game's own HUD icons, and ammunition counts down round by round. The count is marked with a cartridge, a flame for incendiary, or a burst for explosive - while an upgrade is loaded the number is the upgraded rounds left, so reloading does not reset it - and whatever is in your hands is outlined in green.

The [b]Scoreboard[/b] view appears while [b]Tab[/b] is held. The [b]Consistent HUD[/b] view stays visible during play; holding Tab puts it away and shows the scoreboard panel instead, the way the game hides its own survivor HUD. The application watches Tab without changing key binds.

[h1]HOW IT WORKS[/h1]

The overlay does not inject into the game, read memory, hook DirectX, change gameplay, or add server commands. The addon writes live state here and the application reads it:

[code]
left4dead2\ems\overlay_hud\state.json
left4dead2\ems\overlay_hud\ammo.txt
[/code]

The roster file carries every survivor several times a second; the second is tiny and carries only the host's magazine and reserve, twenty times a second, so the ammunition counter follows the trigger.

[h1]CUSTOMISING THE HUD[/h1]

Right-click the overlay's notification-area icon and choose [b]Customize UI...[/b], or hold [b]Tab[/b] and press [b]Insert[/b]. Changes are applied with [b]Save & Apply[/b]; [b]Cancel[/b] discards them.

[list]
[*][b]Scoreboard tab:[/b] the Tab-held panel beside the vanilla scoreboard - position, size, opacity, columns, guide lines, and its own roster filter.
[*][b]Consistent HUD tab:[/b] the HUD kept visible during play - its own roster filter, size, opacity, vertical position, spacing, health numbers, theme, and hotkey.
[*][b]Templates:[/b] [b]Bottom - Horizontal Grid[/b] is the default; [b]Lower Left[/b] and [b]Lower Right Vertical Grid[/b] stack cards in vertical groups. The horizontal layout runs four across and up to three rows, adding columns for larger rosters.
[*][b]Designs:[/b] [b]Basic[/b] uses full cards. [b]Minimalist[/b] puts the name before the health value above a compact five-segment strip and clips long names.
[*][b]Separate You:[/b] moves your card out of the roster to its own side, with a gap that prevents overlap.
[*][b]Weapon HUD:[/b] can be turned off, placed in either bottom corner, stacked or side by side, moved anywhere up the screen, and sized on its own on top of the Consistent HUD's size.
[*][b]Presentation:[/b] health numbers can be hidden, the Consistent HUD can go black-and-white, and card spacing may be negative for intentional overlap.
[*][b]Preview:[/b] the live roster over L4D2, or a simulated one when the game is closed. The editor can also ask the addon to hold the scoreboard open while you position the panel.
[/list]

[h1]WHO THE HUD SHOWS[/h1]

The Scoreboard panel and the Consistent HUD keep separate rosters, each with its own [b]Who to show[/b]:

[list]
[*][b]All survivors[/b] - everyone, including the four the vanilla HUD would draw. Consistent HUD only, since it replaces that HUD.
[*][b]Extra survivors[/b] - mortal soldiers, followers, and plain survivors from slot 5 onward.
[*][b]Mortal soldiers + followers[/b] - Finale Soldiers' mortal soldiers and followers only.
[*][b]Followers only[/b] - soldiers currently following a player.
[/list]

The Scoreboard panel always excludes the original four: L4D2's own scoreboard beside it lists them already. Immortal holdout soldiers are never shown, since they cannot be hurt. Followers carry a blue [b]FOLLOW[/b] marker on mixed rosters.

[h1]IF NOTHING APPEARS[/h1]

[list]
[*]If Compact does not start, install the .NET 9 Desktop Runtime for Windows x64.
[*]Check the top-right badge: it reports when the addon is missing, duplicated, disabled, or out of date.
[*]No badge or HUD at all - use [b]-windowed -noborder[/b] and confirm the application is running.
[*]Panel empty - confirm the session is hosted and that the selected roster filter has survivors to show.
[*]No weapon panel - confirm the addon and the app are the latest versions and that you are the host. It draws the host's own weapons and is never shown while Tab is held.
[*]Enable the [b]debug console[/b] for a readable log of the addon, state file, version, and errors.
[/list]

[h1]COMPATIBILITY[/h1]

Script only. The addon reads survivor state and writes two files; it changes no gameplay, entities, damage, Director behavior, or cvars. The application draws outside the game and does not conflict with HUD replacements.
