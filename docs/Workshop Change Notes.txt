v2.1.3

[h1]v2.1.3 - reinforcements get their own badge[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]REINFORCEMENT badge[/b] - soldiers called in with [b]help![/b] now carry a yellow REINFORCEMENT badge on their card instead of the blue FOLLOW one, so a called-in soldier is never mistaken for one you sent to follow by hand.
[/list]

The badge shows in every roster filter, and stays on a reinforcement that has been killed while its body is still on the map. A reinforcement whose timeout runs out loses its card at that moment, when it turns immortal, rather than when its body finally disappears - an immortal soldier cannot be hurt, so a health card for one says nothing. Soldiers you tell to follow yourself are unchanged and keep the blue FOLLOW badge.

This needs a Finale Soldiers build that has the [b]help![/b] command. Without it nothing changes.

Both halves advance to v2.1.3 - [url=https://github.com/DrStr4Nge147/Left-4-Dead-2-External-HUD/releases]get the matching application here[/url], and close L4D2 before the addon updates.

v2.1.2
================================================================================

[h1]v2.1.2 - the HUD stays away for the end credits[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]End credits[/b] - finishing a campaign no longer brings the Consistent HUD back over the credits. It stays away from the escape, through the credits, and until the next session begins.
[/list]

This was the known issue in v2.1.1. When the rescue vehicle leaves, the game genuinely hands control back - the survivors are unfrozen, the camera is dropped, and everything the addon could see went back to its normal in-game values - while the credits roll on top. The addon now also asks the Director whether the finale has been won, which is the one thing that stays true for the whole roll.

The HUD is deliberately still drawn while you are fighting your way to the rescue vehicle. That is the most dangerous part of the campaign and the last place to take a health display away.

Both halves advance to v2.1.2 - [url=https://github.com/DrStr4Nge147/Left-4-Dead-2-External-HUD/releases]get the matching application here[/url], and close L4D2 before the addon updates.

[h1]v2.1.1 - the hiding switches are in the editor now[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Hide for cinematics[/b] and [b]Hide for menus[/b] are now checkboxes in the overlay's editor, on the [b]Consistent HUD[/b] tab under [b]Showing it[/b]. They were config-file keys before, which made them options nobody could find. Both stay on by default and Reset UI leaves them alone.
[/list]

The addon itself is unchanged in this release; it advances to v2.1.1 so both halves stay on the same version. [url=https://github.com/DrStr4Nge147/Left-4-Dead-2-External-HUD/releases]Get the matching application here[/url], and close L4D2 before the addon updates.

[b]Known issue:[/b] the Consistent HUD comes back for the end credits after a campaign is finished. When the rescue vehicle leaves, the game hands control back and every signal the addon watches returns to its normal in-game values, so the credits are indistinguishable from live play from the outside. Being worked on.

v2.1.0

[h1]v2.1.0 - the HUD leaves when the game's own HUD does[/h1]

The Consistent HUD stands in for the vanilla survivor HUD, so it now goes away wherever that one goes away, instead of being the only thing left drawn over the scene. It comes back on its own every time.

[list]
[*][b]Finale outro[/b] - the panel leaves with the escape and stays away for the report screen and the loading that follows.
[*][b]Chapter end[/b] - it leaves as the saferoom door closes, not seconds later at the loading bar, so the blur, the score panel, and the music play clean.
[*][b]Map-start cinematic[/b] - same treatment at the other end of the chapter.
[*][b]Pause menu and developer console[/b] - the panel goes the moment the menu opens and returns on Return to game.
[/list]

Both are on by default and both can be turned off in the overlay's editor, on the [b]Consistent HUD[/b] tab under [b]Showing it[/b]: [b]Hide for cinematics[/b] and [b]Hide for menus[/b]. Reset UI leaves them alone.

The overlay application advances to v2.1.0 with the addon and both halves are required - [url=https://github.com/DrStr4Nge147/Left-4-Dead-2-External-HUD/releases]get the matching application here[/url]. Close L4D2 before the addon updates; a Workshop pack replaced while the game is running is not reloaded until the game restarts.
